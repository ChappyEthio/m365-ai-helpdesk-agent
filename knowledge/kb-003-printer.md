---
id: KB-003
title: Printer not printing
audience: all
---
First check the printer screen for paper jams or low toner.
On Windows, open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners, select the printer, open the queue and cancel stuck jobs.
Restart the print job. If jobs still stay in the queue after a restart of your laptop, raise a Device ticket with the printer name and floor.
