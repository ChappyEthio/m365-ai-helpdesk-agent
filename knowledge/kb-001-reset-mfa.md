---
id: KB-001
title: Lost phone - how to get back into your account (MFA)
audience: all
---
If you changed or lost your phone, you cannot approve Microsoft Authenticator sign-ins.
Do not create a new account. Contact the IT helpdesk and ask for a Temporary Access Pass (TAP).
IT will verify your identity by video call with your line manager present, then issue a TAP valid for 8 hours.
Use the TAP at https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo to register Authenticator on your new phone.
Resetting MFA methods is a high-risk change and always needs line-manager approval.
