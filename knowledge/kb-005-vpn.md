---
id: KB-005
title: Connecting to the office network from the field (VPN)
audience: all
---
Field staff connect with FortiClient VPN. Use your Microsoft 365 email and password, then approve the sign-in in Authenticator.
If the connection drops on a weak mobile network, switch the VPN to TCP mode and reconnect.
VPN access is only granted to staff who need office file shares; most work runs in SharePoint and Teams without VPN.
