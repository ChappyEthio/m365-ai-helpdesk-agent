---
id: KB-002
title: Requesting access to a shared mailbox
audience: all
---
Shared mailboxes (for example finance@ or procurement@) are owned by a department head.
To get access, raise a ticket with the mailbox name and the reason. The mailbox owner must approve the request.
After approval, IT grants Full Access and the mailbox appears in Outlook within 60 minutes.
Never share your password with a colleague to reach a mailbox; this breaks the data protection policy.
